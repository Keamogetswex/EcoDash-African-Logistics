# EcoDash-African-Logistics


Introduction and Problem Statement:

The occurrence of Load shedding in South Africa has consistently affected various areas of our daily lives.  According to Niselow (2019) from news24 the crisis hit the country for the fist time in November 2007, causing a significant disruptions to many citizen’s daily lives. Although it has not been as severe in most parts of South Africa as of recent for many years load shedding has been one of the biggest issues in South Africa. This project’s objective will be zooming into and providing a solution to one of the most affected aspects of our present daily lives. I will address load-shedding’s affect on deliveries in South Africa. To address the issue at present I will create a load-shedding simulation game of how we, as a country, can overcome this. In this simulation I will follow a green, eco-friendly, approach to how deliveries in South Africa can be successfully carried out in the occurrence of load-shedding. With the use of electronic vehicles that make use of solar energy to recharge. In the model I have also included extra obstacles such as livestock on and off road, potholes as it is also a major issue, road flooding and constructions on and off the road for an accurate life like feel. 

Model Development and Design:

In the model I have applied trigonometry for the movement of the electronic vehicles so that the user will be able to move then how a normal vehicle moves, forward and reverse, in the direction that it is facing. I have also made use of vector math to calculate the distance between the vehicle and the obstacles to have an effect of the vehicle’s movement the closer it gets to it.  The electronic vehicle will store an angle for changes in steering. The distance that the car has travelled which is in the HUD has been calculated with vector math making use of the vehicles previous position and present position. The obstacles have also been put in random places, there has also been a use of rounding down for speed, battery and revenue which is how the game will measure score according to the amount of money made during deliveries. Overall, I have made use of trigonometry for vehicle positioning and direction and math for calculation of distances.
